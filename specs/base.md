MeetingCostCounter (MCC)
========================

MCC is a client-based web-application that does not need to load anything from the internet.

The user enters the number of meeting participants and the hourly rates the project is charged for every participant.
For simplicity, we assume the same hourly rate for each participant.

When data is entered, a "play" button is entered to start the meeting.
A huge counter then shows in real-time how much money was spent during the meeting (i.e., since its start until
the current point in time).

A "stop" button to stop the meeting and display the total of money spent should also be present.
A "pause" button to interrupt and contiune the meeting should also be present.
